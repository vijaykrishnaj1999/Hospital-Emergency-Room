**Description**

Hospital-Emergency-Room

This dashboard analyzing Hospital ER performance : patient volume, wait times, admissionrates, demographics, and satisfication scores for Nov 2023.

Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a monthly analysis of Hospital Emergency Room operations for Nov 2023. It tracks patient volume, wait times, admission rates, demographics, and operational efficiency to improve ER performance and patient care.

Features
1. **KPI Cards**: 
   - No of Patients: 464
   - AVG Wait Time: 35.19 mins
   - Patient Satisfaction Score: 5.09
   - No of Patients Referred: 212
2. **Patients Admission Status**: 
   - Admitted: 231 (49.78%)
   - Not Admitted: 233 (50.22%)
3. **No of patients by Age Group**: Bar chart from 0-9 to 70-79. Peak: 30-39 age group with 68 patients
4. **No of patients by Patient Gender**: Donut chart - Male 51.08%, Female 48.92%
5. **% of Person Seen Within 30 Mins**: Gauge - 197 within target, 267 target missed
6. **No of patients by Day and Hour**: Bar + Heatmap showing peak day is Thursday with 81 patients
7. **No of patients by Department Referral**: General Practice: 91, Orthopedics: 67, Physiotherapy: 19
8. **No of patients by Patient Race**: White: 128, African: 117, Asian: 58
9. **Filters**: Year slicer 2023 and Month Name slicer - Nov

Pages
1. **Monthly View**: Current dashboard with detailed KPIs
2. **Consolidated View**: Overall trends
3. **Patient Details**: Individual patient level data

Dataset
Dataset includes ER patient data with fields: Admission Date, Age, Gender, Race, Admission Status, Wait Time, Department Referral, Satisfaction Score

Tools & Technologies
1. Power BI Desktop (for dashboard design)
2. Power Query (for data cleaning)
3. DAX (for KPIs, % calculations)
4. Excel/CSV (for data source)

Key Insights
- **Patient Volume**: 464 patients visited ER in Nov 2023
- **Wait Time**: Average wait time was 35.19 minutes
- **Admission Rate**: Nearly 50-50 split between admitted and not admitted
- **Peak Times**: Thursdays and 30-39 age group had highest footfall
- **Target Performance**: 197 patients were seen within 30 mins target
- **Top Referrals**: General Practice and Orthopedics are top referring departments
